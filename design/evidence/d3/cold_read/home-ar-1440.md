1. After screen 1 only (about 30 s)
- What it is: an Arabic public reference site about financial inclusion in Yemen, titled "أدلة الشمول المالي في اليمن", whose home page opens with a thesis, not a number: "الشمول المالي في اليمن ليس رقمًا واحدًا." The first paragraph then gives one figure with its caveat in the same breath: "كان لدى 11.9% من البالغين بعمر 15 سنة فأكثر في المناطق التي شملها المسح حساب ... ولم يشمل المسح مناطق يسكنها نحو 23% من السكان."
- Why it looks different from other country-economy sites: no hero image, no KPI tiles, no chart, no map. Instead: a numbered essay outline in the left column ("01 ثلاثة أرقام تقيس ثلاثة أشياء مختلفة", "02 ما الذي لا ينبغي استنتاجه؟", "06 فجوات لا تستطيع الأدلة سدّها بعد"), and a provenance block glued under the number ("متى قيس أو رُصد؟ ... سنة الإبلاغ في بيانات البنك الدولي: 2022؛ دراسة Global Findex 2021؛ العمل الميداني في اليمن من 2022-11-07 إلى 2023-01-09" and "افتح سجل الدليل"). It reads like a methodological dossier or an audit note, which is unusual and, at first glance, more credible than a dashboard. The cost is that it also looks like an essay, not a tool.
- Where I would start: honestly unclear. Screen 1 offers at least five competing entry points with no hierarchy: the nav's first item "استكشف", the outline "01 ...", the underlined record title "أحدث قياس سكاني ممثل متاح لامتلاك الحساب", the bold "افتح سجل الدليل" directly beneath it (two links in the same block that seem to lead to the same place), and the gold sidebar triad "افهم ← استكشف ← تحقّق". With 30 s I would simply keep reading the paragraph; the only link that promises something concrete is "افتح سجل الدليل".
- Also noticed in 30 s: the publisher is nearly invisible. The only brand mark is a tiny, low-contrast Latin "CauseWay" logo top-right; there is no Arabic publisher name in the header. The most prominent institution on the screen is "البنك الدولي", so a skimmer could take this for a World Bank page.

2. After screens 1–2 (about 90 s)
- Yes, bounds are stated, and stated in the sentence that carries the number, which is the strongest thing on the page: "في المناطق التي شملها المسح", "ولم يشمل المسح مناطق يسكنها نحو 23% من السكان", "الأشخاص بعمر 15 سنة فأكثر ضمن التغطية التي شملها المسح.", and for the POS figure "ضمن نطاق الإبلاغ لديه" plus the explicit disclaimer "ولا يثبت الدليل مدى تغطية هذا النطاق لكل مناطق اليمن." Time is bounded too: every number has a "متى قيس أو رُصد؟" line ("مارس 2025 – يناير 2026"). The pull line separates the three numbers by kind: "هذه مقاييس مختلفة: أشخاص، وفجوة داخل مسح واحد، وبنية تحتية؛ ولا يحل أي منها محل الآخر."
- Explorable and verifiable? The page asserts it: four "افتح سجل الدليل" links and the sidebar counter "سجلات الأدلة التي تستند إليها هذه الأرقام (4)". But within two screens I have seen no locator, no source URL, no document title, no dataset name beyond "Global Findex" and "البنك المركزي اليمني – عدن". So I believe the page claims traceability; I have not yet seen evidence of it. A sceptical reader would want at least one visible source pointer on the home page.
- Weak point: the fourth "record", "الشمول المالي منظومة مترابطة، لا درجة واحدة", has no number and its time line is "فترات متعددة عبر أكثر من مجال". Presenting a synthesis under the same "سجل الدليل" label as a survey measurement blurs the very distinction the page is preaching. Layout-wise this block is also awkward: the small gold label "سجلات الأدلة التي تستند إليها هذه الأرقام" sits alone in the far-right gutter, separated from the single card it introduces, while the sidebar says there are four.
- Two precision issues a critical reader notices: "18.35% من الرجال و5.44% من النساء ... بفارق 12.91 نقطة مئوية" gives two decimals from a household survey with partial coverage; on a page that insists on bounds, that reads as false precision. And the same source is named three ways in two screens: "مسح المؤشر العالمي للشمول المالي (Global Findex) لعام 2021", "دراسة Global Findex 2021", "موجة Findex 2021", against a data year of 2022 and fieldwork in 2022–2023. Three years attached to one number is explained in prose but is the kind of thing that will be quoted wrongly.

4. What confused me, labels I did not understand, screenshot risk
- Nav: "المنهجية" has a tiny grey second line "المنهج والقياس" under it, unlike any other item. It looks like a leaked tooltip or a rendering glitch. "استشهد بهذه الصفحة" is rendered in pale grey and reads as disabled. "الأدلة" vs "قراءات الأدلة" vs "البيانات والمصادر" sound like three names for one thing. "أولويات القياس" as a destination is opaque to a newcomer.
- Terminology: "أحدث قياس سكاني ممثل" is a stiff calque of "latest population-representative measurement"; without the shadda "ممثل" can be read as "representative/actor", and "قياس سكاني" is not how an Arabic reader says a national survey (expected: "أحدث مسح تمثيلي للسكان"). "سجل الدليل" (evidence record) does not tell me whether it is a page, a document or a dataset. "إشارات من الأدلة" ("signals") is jargon. "ضمن نطاق الإبلاغ لديه" ends on a dangling "لديه". "البنك المركزي اليمني – عدن" is never explained; a reader who does not know there are two central banks may read it as "the Aden branch". "القيود المُمكِّنة أو المعطلة" (enabling constraints) is self-contradictory in Arabic. "موجة Findex 2021" (wave) is survey-methods jargon.
- Structure: the record title link and "افتح سجل الدليل" appear together under each number, apparently pointing to the same target. The title "الشمول المالي منظومة مترابطة، لا درجة واحدة" appears at least three times (sidebar, screen 2 card, and the boxed section further down). The right-hand label column is mostly empty on screens 3–4 and leaves the page looking lopsided; on screen 2 it produces an orphaned label.
- What a screenshot of screen 1 would get wrong: "11.9%" quoted as "account ownership in Yemen" without the exclusion of areas holding ~23% of the population (it is the last sentence of the paragraph and easy to crop); the year quoted as 2021 while fieldwork is Nov 2022–Jan 2023; "18.35% / 5.44%" quoted as national gender figures with implied two-decimal precision; and the page attributed to the World Bank or an official body, since "CauseWay" is only a small Latin mark and "أدلة الشمول المالي في اليمن" reads like an institutional title. No "last updated" date is visible above the fold.

5. Serious, trustworthy, clear? Is the Arabic natural?
- Serious: yes. Restraint in colour and type, a caveat attached to every number, a section on what the evidence cannot prove, and the crisp chain "الوصول إلى خدمة لا يثبت استخدامها. الاستخدام لا يثبت الجودة أو الأثر. عدد الحسابات لا يساوي عدد الأشخاص." Trustworthy: partly. It performs rigour, but the home page shows no source locators, the publisher is almost anonymous, and the self-description "مورد عام ثنائي اللغة يساعد المستخدمين على فهم أدلة الشمول المالي في اليمن ومقارنتها والتحقق منها ... ولا يتخذ القرارات نيابة عن المستخدمين ولا يحاكي نتائج السياسات" reads like a translated legal disclaimer, not an editorial voice. Clear: the prose is clear sentence by sentence; the page as an object is not (three columns, sticky sidebar with mixed content, many co-equal entry points, very long).
- Direction and mirroring: correct. Header mirrored with "EN" at the far left; outline numbers "01" on the right edge of each item; question numbers on the right; the outline column on the left as the mirror of an LTR right sidebar; Latin runs such as "(Global Findex)" and "Findex 2021" sit correctly inside Arabic lines; the ISO range "من 2022-11-07 إلى 2023-01-09" is not reversed. Nothing looks flipped.
- Numbers: Western digits throughout, consistent, with "%" after the number and "1,473" with a Latin thousands comma. Acceptable for a data site, though a Yemeni print reader would often expect Eastern Arabic digits. The inconsistency is between prose dates ("من نوفمبر 2022 إلى يناير 2023") and machine-style ISO dates in the provenance block ("2022-11-07"); the second looks generated, not written.
- Punctuation and diacritics: good signs of care: Arabic comma and semicolon "،" "؛", "؟", guillemets «الأحدث», tanwin and shadda where needed ("رقمًا واحدًا", "رُصد", "نُفذ", "جُمعت", "تُحدَّث", "المُمكِّنة", "تتبّع").
- Register: the vocabulary is mostly natural ("امتلاك الحساب", "نقاط البيع", "البنية التحتية", "القاعدة التنظيمية"), but sentence shape is English: long periods with parenthetical qualifiers, "لعام 2021 في اليمن، الصادر عن البنك الدولي (نُفذ العمل الميداني ...؛ وسنة البيانات ...)", and label-nouns like "إشارات من الأدلة", "سياق المنظومة", "قراءات الأدلة" that are translations of an English information architecture rather than Arabic headings. It does not read as machine-translated; it reads as carefully translated policy English.
- Typesetting and sidebar: body Naskh with generous leading is comfortable; headings in a heavier face pair well. But the sidebar, the gold labels and the provenance lines are set very small and in grey/gold on white ("سجلات الأدلة التي تستند إليها هذه الأرقام (4)" is barely legible), which hurts Arabic more than Latin at that size. The sidebar is crowded (8 outline items, 4 records, a triad, a paragraph and a nav link) and the right-hand label column is mostly white space. The "CauseWay" mark is Latin-only and faint; there is no Arabic form of the publisher's name in the header.
