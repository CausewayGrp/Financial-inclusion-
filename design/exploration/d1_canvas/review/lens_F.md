# LENS F — product-design critique: signature, genericity, presence

Paths relative to `out/`.

## 1. Per proposition

**T1 · Register**
- Establishes: a real apparatus. Clock, population and ID sit as start-side marginalia, and the Home evidence list becomes a three-column when/what/for-whom register (`fold/T1-record-en-d.png`, `tiles/T1-home-en-d-2.png`, `fold/T1-record-ar-d.png`).
- Genericity: stripped, it is a legislation/journal HTML view under a two-tier institutional nav (`fold/T1-home-en-d.png`); the record's right column is a think-tank data brief.
- MUST-FIX: 8.55 % and +11 % typeset as stat tiles outside their sentence — dashboard metrics, numbers outside their bounded context (`fold/T1-record-en-d.png`, `fold/T1-record-en-m.png`, `fold/T1-record-ar-d.png`, `crops/T1-record-en-primary.png`).
- SHOULD-FIX: desktop first screen ends in an empty band under "Questions to start from" (`fold/T1-home-en-d.png`); "When was it measured or observed?" four times as a margin label (`tiles/T1-home-en-d-2.png`); ID chips as pills (`tiles/T1-record-en-d-2.png`); panel 1 a vertical scatter, dot above square at one x (`crops/T1-reading-en-figure.png`).
- Cold misreading: the record crop reads "+11 % growth", 8.55 % a secondary KPI.

**T2 · Argument**
- Establishes: calm, one measure, generous leading; the boundary keeps label and rule on mobile (`fold/T2-reading-en-m.png`).
- Genericity: centred logo, letterspaced wordmark, centred nav — the magazine masthead; cream ground and Plex Light make it a foundation long-read (`fold/T2-home-en-d.png`, `fold/T2-record-en-d.png`). Nothing in the grammar comes from the evidence problem.
- MUST-FIX: wordmark rendered twice on the mobile first screen, both languages (`fold/T2-home-en-m.png`, `fold/T2-home-ar-m.png`).
- SHOULD-FIX: bold numerals in light text build a number-first scan path 561 → 1,473 → 1,357 → 1,473 → 8.55 % → +11 % (`crops/T2-record-en-primary.png`, `crops/T2-record-ar-primary.png`); Home evidence records and the figure's text alternative collapsed behind "+" (`tiles/T2-home-en-d-2.png`, `crops/T2-reading-en-figure.png`); boundary as a stock left-border callout (`tiles/T2-home-en-d-2.png`).
- Cold misreading: the last bold token, +11 %, is the takeaway.

**T3 · Strata**
- Establishes: labelled, numbered sections; the "Another view" panel nests well (`tiles/T3-home-en-d-3.png`).
- Genericity: white cards on grey with an "on this page" box — design-system documentation / consultancy insights page (`fold/T3-home-en-d.png`, `fold/T3-record-en-d.png`); on mobile the index becomes outlined pills (`fold/T3-home-en-m-2.png`, `fold/T3-record-en-m.png`). Four avoid-list items at once.
- MUST-FIX: the trail does not match the strata — sidebar 01 "Two publications…" versus card 01 "What not to conclude"; sidebar 07 "Trace the evidence" versus card 03 (`fold/T3-reading-en-d.png`, `tiles/T3-reading-en-d-3.png`). The persistent trail is T3's claim and it is broken.
- SHOULD-FIX: the boundary card carries the same weight as every other card (`fold/T3-reading-en-d.png`); vertical scatter as T1 (`crops/T3-reading-en-figure.png`).
- Cold misreading: a "01" card that is not the index's 01 — a section looks missing.

**T4 · Instrument**
- Establishes: the Home first screen is the evidence with its clocks, not an about-paragraph (`fold/T4-home-en-d.png`, `fold/T4-home-ar-d.png`); the Reading opens with the question above the title (`fold/T4-reading-en-d.png`, `fold/T4-reading-ar-d.png`); one boundary voice — teal, double rule, label — on Home, in the Reading and as the figure's "not directly comparable" divider (`tiles/T4-home-en-d-2.png`, `crops/T4-reading-en-figure.png`); panel 1 as two labelled rows on a zero-based horizontal axis keyed by publication (`crops/T4-reading-en-figure.png`, `crops/T4-reading-ar-figure.png`); "Evidence period" before every traced title (`tiles/T4-reading-en-d-4.png`); confident H1, single-tier head, utility links in the footer (`tiles/T4-home-en-d-4.png`). Arabic composed, not merely mirrored (`inspect/T4-record-ar-fluid@320.png`).
- Genericity: stripped, the beige index box is a documentation sidebar and the family is "statistics-office methodology page"; the signature elements are copied from nowhere.
- SHOULD-FIX: index collapses to a pill cloud at 768 and on mobile, items wrapping two per line (`inspect/T4-record-en-fluid@768.png`, `inspect/T4-reading-en-fluid@768.png`, `inspect/T4-record-ar-fluid@768.png`, `fold/T4-reading-en-m-2.png`); semibold on statements, intro, question links and featured reading alike; the phone first screen is a wall of bold (`fold/T4-home-en-m.png`, `tiles/T4-home-en-d-2.png`); five lines of metadata before the record title (`fold/T4-record-en-m.png`); at 320–390 the figure has no value labels on rows or lanes while the alternative is collapsed (`inspect/crops/T4-reading-en-figure@320.png`, `inspect/crops/T4-reading-en-figure@390.png`, `inspect/crops/T4-reading-ar-figure@320.png`).
- Cold misreading: rows ordered 2024-report above 2025-report can still be read top-down as before/after; the row labels are the only defence.

Yemen through materiality: none of the four. Warm neutrals (T2 cream, T3 bands, T4 beige) read as generic; nothing in proportion or rhythm is specific.

## 2. Ranking

1. **T4** — the only proposition whose signature is generated by the evidence problem: question-first head, clock-first objects, boundary voice, row-form panel 1. Generic residue: sidebar box, pills, bold overuse.
2. **T1** — a real apparatus and institutional gravity, betrayed by the stat tiles and diluted across three timid columns.
3. **T2** — calmest and most readable, but restyles the essay/masthead grammar and hides the apparatus.
4. **T3** — restyles documentation cards; its one distinctive claim, the trail, is broken.

## 3. Convergence

Two material things. (a) A screenshot of the figure cannot be read as a fall: rows versus vertical scatter (`crops/T4-reading-en-figure.png` vs `crops/T1-reading-en-figure.png`). (b) Home passes the landing test: the first screen is the instrument answering with clocks, where T1's is an about-paragraph and an empty band (`fold/T4-home-en-d.png` vs `fold/T1-home-en-d.png`). The rest — clock in the record head, boundary rule, trace list — T1 already does or could with small edits; the "verification spine" is the same footer link strip as T1 (`tiles/T4-record-en-d-2.png` vs `tiles/T1-record-en-d-2.png`), and "same order everywhere" holds only on the record — Home and Reading indices are page sections, not the seven questions.

## 4. Three improvements to T4

1. One index grammar at every width: a plain numbered hairline column at ≥1024, a single-column numbered list below — no fill, no borders, no pills (`fold/T4-home-en-d.png`, `inspect/T4-record-en-fluid@768.png`).
2. Weight discipline: semibold only for the "establishes" answer and the boundary voice; everything else regular, hierarchy carried by the label column and scale. Cut the record head to breadcrumb, clock, title; ID below (`fold/T4-home-en-m.png`, `fold/T4-record-en-m.png`).
3. Keep 6,245 and 3,422.16 as row labels and the 2024 lane endpoints down to 320, or open the two-row table by default at phone widths (`inspect/crops/T4-reading-en-figure@320.png`).
