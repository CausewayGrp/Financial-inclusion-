## Ranking
1. **T4** — dignity through apparatus, not decoration; the only Arabic edition that holds on a 320 px phone with links intact; the only figure that survives a screenshot.
2. **T1** — same seriousness in a reference register, tables travel with the figure; but the KPI tiles and broken Arabic link are what this product exists to prevent.
3. **T2** — honest and calm, but light Arabic on cream fails the phone test and nobody here cites an essay.
4. **T3** — strongest verification skeleton, weakest identity: donor-portal idiom plus clipped tables on the phone.

## Convergence
Material but narrow. T4 lets a reader (a) lift the figure and still see two publications of one year, values on each row, not a fall; (b) read the Arabic edition on a phone with link and identifiers intact; (c) cite a record by question number, same order on every surface. Register, clock-first marginalia and in-frame tables T1 already gives; there T4 adds nothing.

## Three improvements to T4
1. Keep value labels at every width: end-of-row values in panel 1 and at least the 2024 endpoint on each lane below 430 px.
2. On the phone the answer precedes the chips (or they collapse into the index); make fluid match fixed.
3. Bidi-isolate every Latin run in Arabic (ISO dates, identifiers, URLs) so "2022-11-07" neither reverses nor breaks; drop the kicker from the index.
