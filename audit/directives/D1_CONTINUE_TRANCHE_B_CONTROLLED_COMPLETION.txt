A. Finance — nominal, USD-equivalent and real value are different concepts
Do not describe conversion of a YER portfolio using the YER/USD market exchange rate as a real deflationunless an appropriate price deflator is separately sourced and justified.
Distinguish:
* nominal YER change;
* USD-equivalent change at a specified exchange rate and date;
* real/purchasing-power change, which requires an appropriate price/inflation basis.
If only the first two are supported, state only the first two.
Preserve the key analytical lesson: nominal portfolio growth does not by itself establish real financial deepening.
B. Formal-credit versus microfinance magnitude
Do not publish a derived microfinance-book/private-sector-credit percentage unless:
* dates align sufficiently;
* stock definitions are compatible;
* institutional universes are understood;
* currency/value basis is compatible;
* overlap/double-counting risk is resolved.
