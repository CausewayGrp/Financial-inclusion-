## 4. What was rejected

- **A new Reading on remittances to households.** There is no current evidence to read; it would be a Reading about an absence that MA-001 already states.
- **Merging CWR-003 into CWR-005.** They answer different questions (units versus durability).
- **Surfacing all ten Readings on all eight domains.** Rejected under binding correction #8.
