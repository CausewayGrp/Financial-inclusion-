5. HOW TO DESIGN IT: THE UNIT FIRST
This site is one evidence unit composed at different scales. The unit defines shared meaning and a consistent order, not a compulsory repeated panel: value, unit, universe, period, publisher, evidence class, what it does not establish, what would settle it, the verification trail. The claim, its essential scope and its material limit always stay together; supporting detail sits at the depth right for Home, a domain, a Reading or a record, through accessible disclosure where needed; qualitative and regulatory evidence keeps its own governed form and is never forced into a numerical template; "what would settle it" appears only where a governed Measurement priority supports it; no field is ever fabricated. Design the unit in Arabic on a phone, then its English sibling. Prove it on CLM-001, /payments/ and Home. Then compose upward: a domain page is its strongest units in order; Home is the strongest units of all, with the system chain; Compare is two units and a verdict; a Reading is an argument with units embedded; /data/ is the unit's source face; the Measurement Agenda is the units that do not exist yet. Propagate only after the unit is proven. The unit's composition, rhythm, disclosure and typography are yours to invent, and they should feel consistent without making Home, a Reading and a record look identical.

Character: calm authority. Hierarchy from type, space, rules and alignment; no card walls, shadows, gradients, photo heroes, stock imagery, decorative charts, icon sets, or motion beyond state changes. V1 tokens; green never means good, gold is never data, states are shown by shape and word, one interactive accent. IBM Plex Sans Arabic and IBM Plex Sans stay the text faces. 6. LANGUAGE: ARABIC AND ENGLISH
- Arabic is designed, not translated. Its measure, leading and line breaks are set independently of English; never fit Arabic into the English column width. No letter-spacing, justification, kashida or synthetic weights. Avoid breaks inside hamza clusters and idafa. The order of qualifiers in a unit is لمن / متى / بماذا / المصدر.
- Bidi: isolate ranges (2022–2023), percentages, currency codes, record IDs and Latin names with bdi or dir, and test real RTL reading order and screen-reader order, not CSS direction alone. Arrow and path direction follows function, never a mirror of the English page. Western digits per the house style; search accepts both.
- Vocabulary: the Master's controlled terms govern. The public Arabic for an Evidence Record is سجل الدليل; الحدود means measurement limits; التشغيل is never implied by الترخيص; المستفيدون are not مستخدمون منتظمون; البنك المركزي اليمني – عدن is named in attribution without generalising scope. Where a term is unsettled, prefer رصد / تقدير / إسقاط, مجتمع القياس, امتلاك حساب, غير متاح / محجوب إحصائيًا / صفر, and record the choice. A percentage-point difference is never written as a growth rate in either language.
- English is idiomatic, economical evidence-report prose: scope and period before the quotable conclusion; issuer attribution kept; no unexplained acronyms, no walls of disclaimers, no implied certainty, no "latest" without the series basis; programme coverage is never national prevalence; adopted, installed, operating, measured use and welfare effect are five different things.
- Protected copy: the Home thesis "Financial inclusion in Yemen is not one number" / «الشمول المالي في اليمن ليس رقمًا واحدًا», "Three figures, three different things measured", the inference-limit paragraph (infrastructure ≠ users, licence ≠ operation, access ≠ use, accounts ≠ people) and "Questions to start from" are RETAIN.
