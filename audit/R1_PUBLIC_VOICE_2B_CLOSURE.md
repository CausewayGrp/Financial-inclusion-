Review lens: institutional neutrality, CauseWay posture, source-vs-synthesis boundary, and unnecessary `strongest / best / should / Yemen needs` language.

## Decision
**KEEP — no Production Master edit required in this bounded session.**

This is a substantive decision, not a skipped edit. After the prior About/Terms surgery, the four reviewed surfaces already sit on the correct side of the institutional boundary.

### Home — KEEP
The page states what the available evidence supports, what it does not prove and how to verify it. Its `What should not be inferred?` wording is an evidence-boundary instruction to the reader, not policy prescription. The page does not claim a current national rate from newer infrastructure data and does not use `strongest/best` posture.

### Explore — KEEP
`The question determines what the system should answer` and `Every question should resolve the same six things` describe the product's own evidence discipline. They do not prescribe national policy or claim authority over Yemen. Rephrasing them would be cosmetic rather than materially more truthful.

### Access — KEEP
`What a reliable national access atlas would require` correctly frames an evidence specification. It does not say what Yemen must do, and the route explicitly preserves provider address ≠ active service point, infrastructure ≠ use and missing geography ≠ zero.

### Providers — KEEP
The route consistently separates listing/licensing from operation, market denominator, geography, activity and outcome. The heading `Money exchangers are part of the financial system, not a footnote` is editorially emphatic, but the body immediately binds the statement to dated OECD/CBY evidence and explicitly says it is not a verified 2026 roster. Changing the heading alone would not materially improve truth or neutrality, so it is retained under the Counterfactual Deletion/Test-of-Materiality rule.

## What became more true
We now have explicit evidence that these four high-traffic surfaces do not require another Master rewrite merely to make them sound more neutral. This prevents unnecessary churn in a mature evidence model.
