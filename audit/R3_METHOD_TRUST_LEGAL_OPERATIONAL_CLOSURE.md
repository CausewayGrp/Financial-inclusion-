# R3 closure — Methodology + public trust / legal / operational copy

## Scope

R3 reviewed and corrected the public Methodology, Rights, Terms, Privacy, Accessibility, Corrections and Contact surfaces. The decision owner was the methodology/operations lead, challenged by rights/legal and accessibility/privacy lenses.

The Production Master remained the sole semantic and publication-state authority. Material public-copy defects were corrected in the Master first and then regenerated into subordinate public projections.

## What became more true

- Accessibility now states WCAG 2.2 as a design/testing reference and target, not as verified conformance. Browser, RTL and assistive-technology acceptance remain explicit release work.
- Privacy now distinguishes known product behaviour from deployment-specific facts. It states that no account is required for reading, records the browser-local language preference, and does not invent hosting-log fields, retention periods, processors, analytics or cookies that have not been verified.
- The public contact path is no longer a release placeholder. The monitored institutional contact is `office@causewaygrp.com`, verified against CauseWay's current official public site. No response-time commitment was invented.
- Rights now explicitly separates evidence fitness, citation, factual use and redistribution. A public citation or locator is not treated as a redistribution licence.
- Terms now describes original publishers as the authoritative source for the data and publications they issue, while CauseWay remains responsible for its own synthesis, derivations and presentation. It does not assign ungrounded legal responsibility to publishers.
- Corrections now distinguishes stable-ID traceability from an unconditional promise that every historical version will remain publicly available.
- Methodology no longer exposes internal production workflow vocabulary such as candidate promotion, source-owner challenge or Production Master integration in public copy.
- Public rights copy no longer implies that a public API exists when the current product is static-first.
- Four English-only shared section-role labels were removed from bilingual operational rows so Arabic pages no longer leak English eyebrow labels.

## What became simpler

The public trust surfaces now read as finished public product copy rather than implementation instructions. Deployment-specific unknowns remain bounded to the facts that genuinely depend on hosting/runtime acceptance.

## What remains deliberately unresolved

R3 does not claim live-release readiness. Exact hosting/security log configuration, any future analytics/cookie configuration, deployed accessibility acceptance and final correction/version behaviour remain subject to later runtime/release acceptance. No privacy retention period, processor, WCAG conformance claim or response SLA was invented.
