## Rule

Each high-value admitted Yemen source is read in four layers, which are never merged:
1. **SOURCE OBSERVATION** — what the source measured or recorded.
2. **SOURCE INTERPRETATION** — what the source's authors conclude or recommend, attributed to them.
3. **CAUSEWAY SYNTHESIS** — how this product uses the source, and with what firewall.
4. **NOT ESTABLISHED** — what the source cannot carry.

Text is paraphrased, never reproduced. Every item already exists in the Master (32_QUAL_LITERATURE QUAL-001…020, claims, evidence records). This file adds no fact.

### 1. World Bank — Global Findex, 2021 wave for Yemen (SRC-WB-FINDEX-AGG-2022; 2025 edition card)

- **Observation.**
  - 11.9% of adults 15+ in covered areas had an account; women 5.44%, men 18.35%; poorest 40% 6.5%, richest 60% 15.5%; primary education or less 6.98%.
  - Fieldwork ran from 7 Nov 2022 to 9 Jan 2023.
  - Areas holding about 23% of the population were excluded, and more than a quarter of sampling units were replaced.
- **Interpretation (World Bank).** None used beyond definitions. The 2025 edition's card states that it creates no newer Yemen estimate.
- **CauseWay synthesis.** The latest *representative* people-side anchor. Levels come before gaps. It is read across evidence clocks (CWR-004, CWR-007; CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-061).
- **Not established.** A 2026 level; causes of the gaps; any governorate map; values for the excluded areas.

### 2. World Bank — Yemen Financial Sector Diagnostics 2024, with the 2022 enterprise survey annex (SRC-WB-FSD-2024-001)

- **Observation (seven selected governorates, 2022-09).**
  - 58.2% of formal firms (191 of 328) held an account.
  - Working-capital shares were 69.12% internal and 0.56% banks.
  - Intermediaries used: money exchangers 73.48%, banks 24.39% (multiple response).
  - Finance was cited as a general challenge by 22%.
  - 91.84% rated finance obstacles major or moderate, in a tabulation that excludes firms reporting no need.
  - 80.18% of 217 informal firms had no account.
- **Interpretation (World Bank).** A diagnostic of sector structure, CBY-Aden, banking, money transfer and exchange, and access to finance. Its conclusions are attributed to the Bank.
- **CauseWay synthesis.** Among formal firms in the sample, holding an account went with almost no bank financing of working capital. Money exchangers were the most frequently reported intermediary in that multiple-response item among the surveyed firms (2022, seven governorates; firms could name more than one, so not market shares). The /firms/ section that states it (PB-0520) is HELD at execution by the literal-closure rule, so it is not yet public (CWR-008). This is the clearest firm-side case that access is not use.
- **Not established.** National rates; a ranking of constraints; market shares; the current situation.

### 3. IMF — 2025 Article IV staff report and its supplement (SRC-IMF-AIV-2025…; 23_REMITTANCES)

- **Observation.**
  - Remittance inflows reported for 2018–2024, including US$1,861.7 million in 2024 (staff calculations).
  - The supplement gives a 2025 estimate of US$1,977 million and projections from US$2,159 million (2026) to US$3,110 million (2030).
- **Interpretation (IMF staff).** The history rests on staff calculations. The projections rest on the programme scenario.
- **CauseWay synthesis.** Two vintages, drawn as a broken line. The labels are "reported history", "estimate" and "projection", never "observed" (LEAD-B06).
- **Not established.** Household receipt or distribution; informal hawala volume; any economic change implied by a forecast revision.

### 4. Central Bank of Yemen — Aden: annual reports AR2024 and AR2025

- **Observation.** The 2024 remittance value is US$6.245 billion in AR2024 and is restated in AR2025 at about US$3.42 billion. Banking positions for the same year are published at higher levels after the January 2023 valuation change.
- **Interpretation (CBY-Aden).** As published. The method crosswalk to the IMF series is not documented (CLM-036).
- **CauseWay synthesis.** Same-year restatement is not economic movement (CWR-001, CWR-002). Paths can agree after normalisation while their levels differ (CLM-037).
- **Not established.** The exact CBY formula; a "correct" level; a universal conversion scalar.

### 6. OECD — *Promoting Economic Resilience in Yemen* (SRC-OECD-YEM-RESILIENCE-001)

- **Observation.** Figures attributed to the review (product holding, mobile access) are **not verified** against its text and are withheld (PB-0165; QUAL-012).
- **Interpretation (OECD, attributed).**
  - Fragmented institutions, supervisory gaps and weak formal access are linked to financial-sector fragility and low trust (QUAL-001).
  - Money exchangers are more accessible than banks and central to remittance and trade finance, with some activity beyond licensed functions (QUAL-002).
  - Consumer-protection implementation and statistical capacity are constrained (QUAL-003, QUAL-004).
  - **Recommendations** (QUAL-005–009): restore correspondent banking; MSME credit facilities and guarantees; FinTech and a sandbox; transaction accounts; targeted financial education.
- **CauseWay synthesis.** Context and mechanism for Readings (e.g. the exchanger role alongside firm evidence). Recommendations are shown as **the OECD's**, never as CauseWay policy or as adopted measures.
- **Not established.** Prevalence; quantified trust; causal effects; that any recommendation has been adopted or has an effect.

### 7. OECD/INFE 2023 international survey (SRC-OECD-INFE-2023)

- **Observation.** Yemen is among the 39 economies, with its data collected within technical-assistance or regional projects (to be registered by PB-0108 as OECD-YEM-018). Yemen's scores are verified from the report (acceptance correction A): overall financial literacy 42 out of 100 (Figure 2.1) and financial well-being 15 out of 100 (Figure 4.1); sample 1,000; fieldwork 11 March–1 April 2023; telephone and face-to-face; the attitude score uses the two required attitude questions (29 OECD-YEM-001/002/018 = CONFIRMED).
- **Interpretation (OECD/INFE).** Cross-country averages (confirmed): well-being 42/47, literacy 60/63.
- **CauseWay synthesis.** A capability measure is a different kind of evidence from account ownership. It sits beside it, never combined with it (PB-0105/0106).
- **Not established.** Any rank or distance to other economies (no ranking is adopted); any population rate beyond the survey sample.
