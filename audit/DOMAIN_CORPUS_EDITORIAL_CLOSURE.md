## 1. THE EIGHT CANONICAL SKELETONS (compact) + STRONGEST ANSWER

**/people/ (QE-002 — who is left behind / measured gaps).** Answer: in the latest representative wave (Findex, Yemen obs. 2022) account ownership is 11.9% and unequal on every comparable dimension — women 5.44% vs men 18.35% (12.91 pp; ~3.4×), poorest-40 6.5% vs richest-60 15.5% (9.00 pp), primary-or-less 6.98%. Unit: % of pop 15+. Universe: Findex 2022 (excludes ~23% of pop; n=1,000). Clock: fieldwork Nov-2022→Jan-2023 (use/save/borrow evidence is 2014). Does-not-establish: a 2026 rate, a governorate map, causes. Measure-next: MA-001/MA-002/MA-003.

**/firms/ (QE-005 — finance severity vs other constraints).** Answer: in the 2022 seven-governorate custom survey, access to finance ranks 6th (22%) below electricity 50%/fuel 46%/transport 32%/tax 30%/instability 27% — yet 91.84% of finance-relevant firms call it major/moderate, and behaviourally firms self-finance (69.12% internal vs 0.56% banks) and transact via money-changers (73.48%) not banks (24.39%). Universe: 328 formal + 217 informal firms, 7 governorates — NOT national. Does-not-establish: national ranking; 91.84% ≠ share of all firms; intermediary % ≠ market share. Measure-next: MA-004/MA-008.

**/finance/ (QE-006 — microfinance: borrowers, savers, portfolio, provider structure).** Answer: the four measures move DIFFERENTLY — borrower reach peaked ~124,917 (Mar-2015) then fell/stagnated (~88,445 end-2020; ~78,686 end-2023 secondary), while savers (→3.3m 2023, accounts not people) and nominal portfolio (→~YER 44bn) rose far more, concentrated on the saver side (Al-Kuraimi). Universe: SFD/SMED + YMN member/reporting panels (9–11 providers) — NOT national census, NOT the CBY-licensed MFB list. Does-not-establish: unique people; real credit deepening; a 2026 level. Measure-next: MA-004/MA-008.

**/providers/ (QE-008 — who is evidenced, under whose authority, what status).** Answer: formal status by class/date under CBY-Aden authority, NOT operation — 26 licensed banks (12 classified microfinance; verified 2026-09-07); 2026 exchange/remittance roster = 98 companies + 225 establishments + 106 agents (429 rows, not deduplicated, count-only); e-wallets 7/9/8 across dated points (no clean series); 2026 enforcement Decisions suspend/close named entities (dated). Does-not-establish: operation, branches, market share, permanence of an enforcement status. Measure-next: MA-005/MA-007.

**/payments/ (QE-004 — digital finance: durable use vs infrastructure/transactions).** Answer: the data still mainly measure infrastructure/registration/transactions, not durable use — POS terminals 561→1,473, transactions 8,015→24,026, value YER 320m→1.262bn (Mar-2025→Jan-2026), e-wallet subscribers 414,631→581,075→[universe break]→2,102,484; none is a validated unique-repeat-user measure; Findex people-side ownership is 11.9%. Does-not-establish: inclusion rate, merchant adoption, unique/repeat/voluntary use. Measure-next: MA-002/MA-009/MA-010/MA-007.

**/remittances/ (QE-007 — flows → household connection).** Answer: at household level, current data say very little — macro flows are sizeable and reconstructed (IMF external-sector observed 2018 $1,329.2m → 2024 $1,861.7m; 2025 est $1,977m; 2026–30 proj → $3,110m) and corridor costs are known (Q3-2025 KSA→YEM 1.26–2.70%, UAE→YEM 5.62–7.53%), but household receipt/channel evidence is a single 2014 survey (18.3% received domestic remittances). Does-not-establish: informal/hawala volume, household distribution, payment-system throughput; observed≠estimate≠projection; a revision≠an economic change. Measure-next: **none currently bound (defect).**

**/reforms/ (QE-003 — reforms/infrastructure → access/use/quality/outcomes).** Answer: a dense datable 2023–2026 reform record (e-money rules + 2025 FX amendment; FCP 2023; FMIIP project 2025; YPCC founded Aug-2026; defaulters registry; SMP 2026) — but every item sits at an early state (rule/funding/institution/implementation); the FMIIP results framework baselines the new rails at zero (FPS operational=No, participants=0; RTGS operational=No, participants=0; cleared FPS transactions baseline 0). Real at the rule/rail end; unmeasured — **not failed** — at the use/outcome end. Does-not-establish: adoption, coverage, reliability, redress performance, inclusion outcome; institution founded ≠ live service; procurement ≠ go-live. Measure-next: MA-010/MA-006/MA-002/MA-008/MA-009.

**/access/ (QE-009 — where reachable / where blank).** Answer: evidence establishes fragments (national POS totals; provider rosters; a 2016–2019 five-provider study ~93.8% urban) but NOT, consistently, which access points operate at a given place/date — no verified geocoded operating-point network exists, so a national access map does not yet exist; blank = "not observed", not zero. FMIIP access points 817 (Jan-2025) → target 1,021 (2030) is an aggregate KPI, not a map. Does-not-establish: geographic coverage, operation at named locations; listed address ≠ active service point; KPI ≠ map; target ≠ result. Measure-next: MA-005/MA-010.

## 4. SYSTEM VIEW — financial inclusion as one interconnected system (not 8 silos)

The domains are not independent indicators; the evidence links them, and the product should make the links visible while preserving each measurement's limit:
- **People ↔ Firms:** households (informal borrowing from family/relatives) and firms (self-finance + money-lenders/relatives) both route around banks — the same absence of a functioning formal credit/deposit channel.
- **Firms ↔ Providers/Remittances:** firms transact via **money-changers (73.48%)**, tying firm finance directly to the exchange/remittance sector — the strongest cross-domain link, currently unmade.
- **Providers → Access → Payments → Use:** provider legal status (listing/licence) sits upstream of operating geography (access) upstream of infrastructure (payments) upstream of durable use — each arrow is a place where the evidence currently stops (licence≠operation≠reachable≠used).
- **Reforms → the missing middle:** rules and rails (National Switch, FPS/RTGS, YPCC) are the origin of the transmission chain; the measured-use and measured-outcome states are empty (baseline zeros) — the "missing middle" the product must show as *unmeasured, not failed*.
- **Remittances/Macro → Households:** sizeable reconstructed macro flows and known corridor costs, but the household-connection link is a 12-year-old survey — a measured-flow / unmeasured-household asymmetry.
- **Currency bifurcation (context)** runs under everything (nominal≠real for portfolio; FX losses for firms; corridor costs) — context that materially changes interpretation, used only where it does.

This is the spine of the Home/system synthesis (QE-001) and reading CWR-009 ("from rail to result: the missing middle").
