# CLOSE-6E domain challenger lens (11 October 2026) — report as delivered

Read-only review of the staged CLOSE-6E texts against the v5 instruction (§5, §8) and the reads V3, V4 and V5. Hats: MSME
finance specialist (6C-1); humanitarian cash / FCV specialist (6C-2); remittance-price statistician (6C-3); central-bank
regulator / legal librarian (6C-4); MSME and microfinance specialist (6C-5). Disposition: DISPOSITION_6E.md.

## BLOCKER

- **B1** P2 (CLM-045 limitations, CWR-010 s6, MA-005, MA-009): the "Pressure" cash-out statements concern humanitarian
  multi-purpose cash assistance (MPCA) programmes in 2026 (V5 §B: "possible effects on MPCA programming"; fn 31 "KII with
  MPCA program manager April 2026"). Written as generic "cash assistance" right after UNICEF's UCT and the G2Px pilot, a
  reader takes the vouchers, agent shortages and e-wallet pilot to be the UCT's, which s6 says made no payment after January
  2025. Name MPCA as a programme separate from the UCT, in all four places.
- **B2** P3: RMO-RPW-US-2025Q3-200/-500 carry calculation_state SOURCE_PUBLISHED_AVERAGE and evidence_type
  PRIMARY_OFFICIAL_CORRIDOR_QUOTE_AVERAGE while their caveat says "computed here … the corridor page was not read". Use a
  computed state; universe "in the dataset", not "displayed".

## SHOULD-FIX

- **S1** "sub-districts that the studies place in areas under the internationally recognized government": V5 §C has no such
  placement for SAM.
- **S2** "point to debt repayment as an early use of transfers" is stronger than either study; SAM's 40% is regular
  expenditure over 30 days across households paid and not paid in that window (about 47% and 30%); Cash Duration measures
  what "improved the most at the endline". Write "a common use" and give the split.
- **S3** SAM "published in November 2024" rests on the upload path only (PDF created 1 August 2024; no printed date).
  Write "posted online November 2024"; give reference periods in evidence_period.
- **S4** MA-009 drops "announced reach"; "October 2024 cycle" imprecise (18th cycle, to mid-October).
- **S5** "the report names neither the programme nor the areas": the report frames the crisis in IRG areas, rural recipients
  most affected, and says the problems eased after the high-demand period.
- **S6** MA-001 "shop or supplier credit": SAM is customers buying on credit from shops; "supplier credit" is CLM-062's
  firm-to-firm measure.
- **S7** CLM-062 "does not represent informal firms" omits the design and skew: quota sample of 2–4 market areas per
  governorate, first consenting owner, no weights; 179 of 217 firms in Houthi-labelled areas (Fig. 101, p.141).
- **S8** "The last independent measure": "last" and "independent" not established; the discontinuation was from memory in
  V4 and no read file recorded the statement. Write "the most recent published measure this resource holds" and record the
  read.
- **S9** "1.3% of adults (216,245 individuals and firms)": people ≠ firms; give 211,476 individuals and 4,769 firms and the
  1.3% as the profile's expression.
- **S10** YSC-030 fact_ar «ولا يستند … إلى أي أداة تنظيمية» says "rests on", not "cites"; the source names unnamed
  «ضوابط تنظيمية».
- **S11** "The registry adds a platform to the credit-information rules" contradicts "does not link the registry to these
  rules"; add that the page does not say who keeps the notification list.
- **S12** IFAD summary: the figures are the evaluation team's estimates from CACB information (fn 101); Raymah's "<1%"
  assumes 48,000 households (fn 102); restore "in contradiction to Islamic precepts" (para 138).
- **S13** US average compared "at the same transfer sizes" ignores product mix (2 of 12 quotes cash at agent: 4% and 7.5% at
  US$200); the Reading's s6 lacks "computed here".
- **S14** The US source's workbook Terms of Use reads "Restricted Data"; check the licence before release.
