## New findings

### NB1. BLOCKER [EVIDENCE]: the route description says the channel "is not measured"

- **Quote:**
  - `meta_description_en`: "In Yemen's Findex 2021 wave, 31.9% … and 11.9% had an account; the channel is not measured."
  - AR: «…؛ ولم تُقَس قناة وصول المال.»
- **Where it comes from:** `audit/close_out/drafts/CWR-012_draft.json` L369–370. It is the same text as in v1, but the v1 bundle did not show it, so this is the first time I have reviewed it.
- **Evidence:**
  - VIS-FINDEX-FLOW-CHANNELS, limitations: "the World Bank publishes most channel values for all adults only". So the survey did collect channel data; this resource simply holds no value for recipients.
  - CLM-026 lists 14 measures that were asked but have no published value.
  - CORE_CONSTITUTION, Evidence discipline 5: "Restricted does not mean missing."
  - The Reading body itself says "no channel value for that wave is held here".
- **Failure:** this is a false public statement in the text that appears in search results and on cards, and it contradicts both a governed record and the Reading.
- **Replacement:**
  - EN: "In Yemen's Findex 2021 wave, 31.9% of adults in surveyed areas received domestic remittances and 11.9% had an account; no channel value for that wave is held here."
  - AR: «…وكان لدى 11.9% من البالغين حساب؛ ولا تتضمن قاعدة الأدلة قيمة لقنوات الحوالات في تلك الموجة.»

### NS1. SHOULD-FIX [CBY]: the s3 rule sentence does not say who the rule binds, and its tense asserts it is still in force

- **Quote:**
  - EN: "Since 26 June 2024, a CBY-Aden decision has required exchange companies, exchange establishments and money-transfer agents…"
  - AR: «ومنذ 26 يونيو 2024 يُلزم قرار … شركات ومنشآت الصرافة ووكلاء الحوالات…»
- **Failure:**
  - A supervisor in Sana'a reads this as binding every exchange company in Yemen.
  - "Has required" and «يُلزم» also claim the rule is still in force, which no record shows.
- **Replacement:**
  - EN: "On 26 June 2024, a CBY-Aden decision required the exchange companies, exchange establishments and money-transfer agents under its supervision to execute…"
  - AR: «وفي 26 يونيو 2024 ألزم قرار صادر عن البنك المركزي اليمني – عدن شركات ومنشآت الصرافة ووكلاء الحوالات الخاضعين لرقابته بتنفيذ…»
