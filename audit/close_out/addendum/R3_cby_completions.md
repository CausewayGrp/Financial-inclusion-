«1. الحوالات الخارجية الواردة بالريال السعودي:
أ- يتم تسليم الحوالات للمستفيدين بالريال السعودي عبر فروعكم أو عبر وكلاءكم الفرعيين المرخصين من البنك المركزي.

ج- يمكنكم شراء مبلغ الحوالة من الوكيل الفرعي مقابل ريال يمني بما لا يتجاوز سعر بيع الريال السعودي المعتمد للتجار من وحدة التعاملات بالنقد الأجنبي بالبنك المركزي.»

**[translation]**
- (a) Transfers are paid to beneficiaries in Saudi riyals, through your branches or through your sub-agents licensed by the Central Bank.
- (b) Where a sub-agent pays the beneficiary, the transfer amount is paid to the sub-agent in Saudi riyals, with the commission
  set by the foreign bank or company providing the service. An incentive of no more than one quarter of one per cent (0.0025) of
  the transfer amount may be added.
- (c) You may buy the transfer amount from the sub-agent against Yemeni rials, at no more than the Saudi-riyal selling rate for
  traders approved by the Central Bank's Foreign Exchange Dealing Unit.

**[translation]** "External transfers received in Yemeni rials (external transfers priced in Yemeni rials):

### Other provisions (summary; Arabic wording above is verbatim)

- §5: no extra advantage may be given to sub-agents, such as buying the amount and reselling it cheaper, or buying against dollars.
  Sub-agents may not claim an external balance.
- §6: a main agent may fund another main agent's external balance, with a commission of no more than 0.0025.
- §7: no commission may be given to the foreign sender or the local beneficiary.
- §8: no sub-agent user accounts («يوزرات») may be given to unlicensed parties.
- §9 (verbatim): «اتخاذ العناية الواجبة في التحري عن مواقع تسليم الوكلاء الفرعيين للحوالات الخارجية الواردة، وعدم تسليمهم عملات أجنبية خارج مناطق المجلس السياسي الأعلى مقابل حوالات مسلمة للمستفيدين داخل مناطق المجلس السياسي الأعلى.»
  **[translation]** "Exercise due diligence on where sub-agents pay out incoming external transfers, and do not hand them foreign
  currency outside the Supreme Political Council areas against transfers paid to beneficiaries inside those areas."
- §10: periodic data are to be provided to the Banking Supervision Sector and the Foreign Exchange Dealing Unit. Violations count
  as «أعمال التحايل والمضاربة في العملة» **[translation]** "fraud and currency speculation".
