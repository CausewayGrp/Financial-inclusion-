# Economic and conflict context — usage policy

**Status:** `VERIFIED_BY_CLAUDE_TEAM` · `INDEPENDENT_OPENAI_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED`

## 1. The test

Macro, conflict or humanitarian context may enter a page, Reading or caption only if it answers one question: **"Why does this change the interpretation of this financial-inclusion evidence?"** If it only adds atmosphere, it is deleted.

**CONFLICT CONTEXT ≠ CAUSAL PROOF.** Conflict is never offered as the explanation of an observed result unless a source has established that link. None has.

## 2. Context that passes the test (keep, where it is used)

| Context | Why it changes interpretation | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Central-bank authority divided since 2016 | Lists, decisions and statistics carry the issuer's scope. "Official" means CBY-Aden for this corpus. Absence from a list is not "unlicensed elsewhere". | /providers/ "Whose list is this?" (PB-0216); /methodology/ (PB-0613); every CBY reference (PB-0210–0215, 0513, 0580A–0585A) |
| Rial valuation differs between monetary areas; dual exchange rates since 2019 | A nominal rial series is read with its valuation basis. An FX conversion is not a real adjustment. The microfinance portfolio has no recorded valuation basis. | /finance/ (PB-0120–0123); /methodology/ (PB-0613) |
| Survey coverage excludes areas holding about 23% of the population; more than a quarter of sampling units replaced | The latest people-side measure is not national in coverage, and no governorate map may be drawn from it | /people/ (PB-0311/0312); CLM-025; /methodology/ |
| January-2023 valuation change in banking statistics | A published jump can be revaluation, not lending | CWR-002; /finance/ credit (PB-0160) |
| Liquidity and cash-access pressures (2025–2026 rapid evidence) | Explains why delivery and cash-out constrain use after transfers. Never an incidence rate. | CWR-010 context (QUAL-018) |
| The IMF external-statistics reconstruction (2025) | Explains why vintages differ | /remittances/, CWR-001 |

## 3. Context that fails the test (do not insert)

- **General conflict descriptions:** casualties, frontlines, humanitarian totals. They do not change how any governed figure is read.
- **"Because of the war" explanations** for low account ownership, the gender gap or firm constraints. Not established (CWR-007, MA-003).
- **Conflict heat maps or territorial-control maps** laid over financial data (§23 forbidden object).
- **Macro aggregates** (GDP, inflation) without a stated use in a specific comparison. The product has no sourced price deflator in the evidence base, so no real-value claim is made.

## 4. Writing rule

- **Structure:** context → the specific interpretive consequence → the limit.
- **Example:** *"Since 2016 central-bank authority has been divided; the lists on this page come from CBY-Aden instruments, so status under any other authority is not established here."*
- **Tone:** neutral and scope-defining. A statement of evidence scope is not a judgement about either authority. Arabic and English are held to the same neutrality.
