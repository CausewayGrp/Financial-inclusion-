# Support and partnership readiness (stewardship note — not public, not a Design input)

**Status:** internal stewardship note for CauseWay (directive D7 §F7). It is never published on the site, never passed
to Design as a brief, and has no authority over any evidence, wording, finding, Reading, priority or source decision.
Nothing here states or implies how the resource is or will be funded: CauseWay's identity and funding statement is an
owner input (TRUST-09) and is not invented here.

## 1. Public-good proposition

A bilingual, question-first public resource that lets anyone — a Yemeni reader, a journalist, a regulator, a provider, a
researcher, a development partner — find the strongest defensible answer the evidence supports on financial inclusion in
Yemen, see what it does not establish, and trace every figure to its original source in Arabic or English. Its value is
the discipline, not the numbers: people ≠ accounts, access ≠ use, target ≠ result, missing ≠ zero, chronology ≠
causality, in both languages, on every page.

## 2. Editorial-independence covenant

These rules bind CauseWay and any supporter, partner or data provider. They are a condition of any support.

1. **The Production Master is the only authority.** No supporter reviews, approves, delays or vetoes content before
   publication. A supporter may report an error through the public corrections route like anyone else.
2. **Findings are not shaped for funding.** No finding, Reading, Measurement priority, boundary, caveat, source choice or
   wording is added, softened, removed, reordered or emphasised to attract, retain or please a supporter.
3. **No sponsored content.** No supporter's programme, product, institution or result is featured, ranked or framed
   because of support. Programme evidence is treated like any other: programme KPI ≠ national prevalence.
4. **Disclosure.** Every supporter and data partner, and the nature of the support, is disclosed publicly once the owner
   approves a funding statement; undisclosed support is not accepted.
5. **Corrections apply to everyone.** A supporter's own publications are checked and corrected with the same rules.
6. **Walk-away right.** CauseWay may decline or end support that conflicts with these rules; the resource and its
   history stay public.

## 3. Conflict rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| A supporter is also a source (e.g. publishes data the resource uses) | Disclose the relationship next to the source; its figures get no special status; the same verification and boundaries apply |
| A supporter's programme is evidenced on the site | Programme evidence stays labelled as programme evidence; no outcome is claimed beyond what an evaluation establishes |
| A supporter asks for a change | Handled only as a correction report with evidence; decided by the Production Master process and recorded |
| A supporter is a regulated provider or a regulator | No endorsement, ranking or status confirmation; licence/listing ≠ operation stays visible |
| A reviewer or editor has a conflict | They declare it and step back from that decision |

## 4. Three-year maintenance — cost categories (no amounts)

Amounts need owner input; only the categories are listed so that a budget can be built honestly.

1. **Evidence currency:** tracking new releases (Findex, enterprise surveys, CBY-Aden payment reports and circulars, IMF
   and World Bank documents), adjudicating them and updating the Master through transactions.
2. **Bilingual editorial work:** Arabic and English review of every change; periodic whole-corpus acceptance (as F5).
3. **Verification and corrections:** reading original sources, handling correction reports, keeping the correction log.
4. **Engineering and hosting:** static hosting, domain, TLS, security headers, dependency updates, CI, backups.
5. **Accessibility and quality assurance:** audits against WCAG 2.2 after implementation and after major changes.
6. **Rights and source management:** assessing reuse terms, keeping public locators working, archiving source versions.
7. **Measurement agenda follow-through:** scoping the priorities with data holders (see §6); no data collection is
   implied.
8. **Governance:** independent review of major changes; publication of the funding and independence statement.

## 5. Use and impact framework

Report what can be observed, never inflate it into outcomes.

| Level | What may be reported | What may not be claimed |
|---|---|---|
| Activity | Records added, sources checked, corrections made, releases | That the evidence improved decisions |
| Reach | Aggregate visits and downloads (only if an approved, privacy-minimal analytics policy exists — none does now) | Unique people reached, or who they are |
| Use | Documented citations, references in public documents, correction reports received | Influence on a specific decision without evidence from the decision-maker |
| Outcome | Only what an independent evaluation establishes | Any change in financial inclusion attributed to the resource |

## 6. Support and data-partnership menu

- **Core stewardship support** for the categories in §4, under the covenant in §2.
- **Measurement partnerships** with data holders for the ten Measurement priorities (for example a current representative
  demand-side survey wave, reconciled provider-level panels, an operating access-point register), where the partner keeps
  ownership of its data and the resource publishes only what the partner's terms allow, with provenance.
- **Source-access agreements** to obtain public locators or reuse permissions for material now withheld (for example the
  residual-model value behind CLM-044) — the evidence frontier stays explicit until then.
- **Independent review** (Arabic editorial, statistical, accessibility) commissioned under the covenant.
- **Translation of method**, not of findings: training or documentation on reading the evidence, clearly separate from
  the evidence itself.

## 7. Rights and open-content boundary

- **Original sources** keep their own terms. Their reuse terms have not been assessed (`rights_state: NOT_ASSESSED` on
  every source record); the site links to sources and does not republish them.
- **CauseWay's own synthesis, derivations and analysis** are licensed under CC BY 4.0: the owner adopted it on
  3 October 2026 and closed the rights question on 10 October 2026 (`audit/OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-10-10.md`, OWN-04-R).
  `/rights/` and `/terms/` state it in both languages. The licence covers CauseWay's text, analysis, visual designs, the
  compiled records, and the structure and annotations of the exports. It does not cover third-party material, and no
  licence may be implied for it; it does not cover the CauseWay name, logo and marks. No licence file is added: the
  licence is governed content, stated on the pages. One release step remains and it is counsel's, not the owner's
  decision: CauseWay's counsel confirms the CC BY 4.0 text before the first deploy publishes those pages
  (`docs/RELEASE_RUNBOOK.md` step 7a); `licence_text_confirmed` and `public_downloads` stay false until then. No rights
  clearance, legal review or certification is claimed. This is the **reuse** licence (Arabic رخصة), not **regulatory**
  licensing (Arabic ترخيص): CauseWay is not a licensed financial institution and claims no such status.
- **Code** (build, generator, gates) is outside the CC BY 4.0 licence and could be released under an open-source
  licence — still an owner decision, and nothing is decided about it.
- **Sensitive material** is never published: no personal data, no beneficiary-level records, no precise sensitive
  locations, no private locators.
